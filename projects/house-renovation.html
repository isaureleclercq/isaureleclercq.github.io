Design details

The exterior.

The model shows how the roof forms, walls, and entrance fit together.

  1. Intersecting rooflines

    The pitched roof forms overlap at different heights, making the separate volumes of the house visible from above.

  2. A glazed entrance

    Tall glass panels sit beneath a projecting roof, creating a distinct entrance along the darker exterior walls.

  3. Surface & silhouette

    Linear detailing gives the walls texture, while the roof edges and openings define the house’s outline.
